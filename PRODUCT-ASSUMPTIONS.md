# PRODUCT ASSUMPTIONS – BHARATBRIDGE

### 1. What product are we planning to build?

We are developing BharatBridge, a digital workspace for small Indian D2C brands and artisan-led businesses. It helps them organize their product details, explore international markets, track preparation progress and create profiles for potential overseas buyers.

### 2. What problem does our product solve?

Small Indian businesses often find it difficult to understand and organize the steps involved in exploring international markets. Information about products, target markets, preparation tasks and estimated costs can be scattered across different sources. BharatBridge brings these activities together in one platform.

### 3. Who are our target users?

Our primary users are small Indian D2C brands and artisan-led businesses interested in exploring international opportunities. This includes emerging businesses selling handicrafts, home décor, lifestyle products and other Indian-made goods.

### 4. What is the main use case?

A business owner uses BharatBridge to manage product information, explore potential overseas markets, track preparation tasks, estimate costs and create a professional buyer-facing profile.

### 5. What are the core features?

- **Dashboard:** Overview of business information and progress.
- **Product Management:** Add, search and manage product details.
- **Market Explorer:** Explore sample information about the UAE and USA markets.
- **Readiness Centre:** Track preparation tasks through a checklist.
- **Cost Planner:** Calculate illustrative costs, revenue and profit.
- **Buyer Profile:** Create and preview a professional business profile.

### 6. What assumptions are we making about users?

We assume users have basic knowledge of their products but may have limited experience with international markets. They are interested in exploring overseas opportunities and prefer a simple platform to organize their preparation activities.

### 7. What is included in the MVP?

The MVP includes an interactive dashboard, product management, UAE and USA market exploration, a readiness checklist, an illustrative cost planner and a buyer profile creator. It uses sample data and browser local storage to demonstrate the main features without a backend.

### 8. What is excluded from the MVP?

The MVP does not include actual export transactions, shipping bookings, live customs or tax calculations, official compliance verification, payment processing or direct buyer communication. Backend integration, authentication and advanced AI recommendations are also excluded from this version.
